A free photo and video library for Linux and Mac. Your photos never leave your computer, and neither do their coordinates.

Free for any use · Linux and macOS · version 2.0.0

The map is the way into your library.

Open it and see everywhere you have been, as pins. Rest on a pin and its albums slide in beside the map. Zoom from a country to a city to a street corner.

Say where you were

Most photos carry no location. Right-click an album, type the place, and every photo in it lands on the map at once. Thousands of photos in a few clicks.

Named without asking anyone

Piklin carries a list of the world's towns on your own disk. Turning a pin into a city name never sends a coordinate anywhere.

Countries and cities, counted

See how many countries and places you have visited, with the cities listed inside each country.

A whole photo studio, and it stays yours.

Organize, edit and share your memories without an account, a subscription or a cloud.

Made for big libraries

Tested with 150,000 photos on a normal computer. Browsing stays instant, and Piklin stays light on memory.

RAW photos and video

22 RAW formats from Nikon, Canon, Sony and more, plus a full video player and editor.

Edit without fear

Tune, curves, looks, crop and perspective. Your originals are never touched.

Make photos smaller, safely

Free up disk space with compression that checks the result looks the same as the original.

Backups that match exactly

Back up to a drive, a NAS or a cloud you own. A restore returns your library exactly as it was.

Import from anywhere

Cameras, iPhones and iPads, folders and drives, on Linux and on Mac.
